About Olive & Amber

Welcome to Olive & Amber, where every meal tells a story and every moment becomes a beautiful memory. We believe that dining is more than just food; it is about bringing people together and creating unforgettable experiences.

Inspired by nature, fresh ingredients, and rich flavours, we have created a space where elegance meets comfort. Every dish is prepared with care, passion, and attention to detail to give our guests something truly special.

From peaceful lunches to memorable dinners and special celebrations, Olive & Amber welcomes everyone with warmth, wonderful flavours, and genuine hospitality.

Our vision is simple: to make every visit special and every guest feel at home.
